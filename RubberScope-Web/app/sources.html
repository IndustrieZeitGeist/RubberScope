Sources for the image analysis

English notes on what each source covers and how RubberScope applies it. Links lead to the original publications or official documentation.

  • Defines the red, green and blue luma weights. Conversion from higher bit depths is specific to RubberScope. ITU-R BT.601

  • Introduces multiscale filtering based on Hessian eigenvalues. RubberScope defines its own scales and crack thresholds. Frangi et al. (1998), Multiscale Vessel Enhancement Filtering

  • Documents the frangi and apply_hysteresis_threshold functions, including the two connectivity thresholds. scikit-image, Filters

  • Explains opening, closing and black hat. RubberScope selects the shape and size of each structuring element. OpenCV, Morphological Transformations

  • Documents Gaussian smoothing for noise reduction and estimation of the local background. OpenCV, Smoothing Images

  • Documents image moments. RubberScope uses second central moments to estimate the principal axes and orientation. OpenCV, Image Moments

  • Describes thinning binary shapes to digital centre lines. Zhang & Suen (1984), A Fast Parallel Algorithm for Thinning Digital Patterns

  • Discusses length and perimeter estimation on a discrete pixel grid. RubberScope applies a corrected count of neighbouring edges. Kulpa (1977), Area and Perimeter Measurement of Blobs in Discrete Binary Pictures

  • Section 5 covers propagation of uncertainty. RubberScope reports the pixel-calibration contribution for area and length density. JCGM 100:2008, Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement

  • Describes the OME data model. RubberScope reads PhysicalSizeX and its length unit for pixel calibration. Open Microscopy Environment, OME Model

  • Discusses precision, recall and F-measures. RubberScope validation obtains the confusion counts by comparing image pixels with a reference mask. Sokolova & Lapalme (2009), A Systematic Analysis of Performance Measures for Classification Tasks
